# Benji & Co. — launch plan for a pet-portrait + digital-download side business

*For Dom, Melbourne FL. Written Oct 10, 2026. Sources are listed at the bottom; anything marked **estimate** is my guess, not a published number.*

## The short version
- **What you sell:** custom pet portraits made from the customer's photo (AI-stylized, checked and touched up by you). Sell them as a digital file, or printed on a canvas, poster, mug or blanket by a print-on-demand (POD) partner who ships it for you. Add a few cheap instant-download printables (pet care planner, coloring pages) as low-effort extra products.
- **Benji is the mascot and your first sample.** Every style you offer gets a Benji version, which becomes your listing photos.
- **Start on Etsy, using Printify.** Add a Shopify site once you have about 30 orders and 10+ reviews.
- **Startup cost:** about **$150–$250** without an LLC (details below).
- **Time:** about **6–10 hours a week** at the start. Most of it goes to answering messages and checking portraits, not to "creating art".
- **Be honest with yourself:** pet portraits are one of the most crowded categories on Etsy. You win by being fast (a proof within 24–48 hours), good at revisions and photogenic in your listings, not by being cheap. Expect slow months at first. The game sim (below) is optimistic about traffic.

## 1. Niche and positioning
- **Positioning:** "Your dog or cat as a classic painting, proof in 24–48 hours, unlimited small tweaks, printed and shipped in the US." The proof speed and the human check are what separate you from one-click AI apps that sell $19.99 files.
- **Pick 3 styles to start, not 12:** for example Renaissance/royal, watercolor, and bold pop-art. Make Benji in all three.
- **Strong gifting moments:** birthdays, "gotcha day", Mother's/Father's Day for pet parents, Christmas (Oct–Dec is your peak) and pet memorials. Memorials need gentle wording and fast turnaround.
- **Later expansion: Space Coast designs** (launch posters, rocket mugs). Use your own original rocket art. **Do not use the NASA "meatball" or "worm" logos, SpaceX/Blue Origin marks, or photos you don't own.** NASA's insignia are protected and need permission for commercial use.

## 2. Which POD partner: Printify vs Printful
Base costs from public listings, Oct 2026 (US shipping in brackets):

|---|---|---|
| Canvas 12x16 | $11.26 (+$9.19) | $25.75 |
| Canvas 16x20 | from $11.26 listed; budget ~$15–16 (**estimate**) | $31.25 |
| 11oz white mug | $4.69, $3.50 with Premium (+$4.39–$7.99) | $5.95 (+$6.69) |
| 18x24 poster | $4.73 (+~$6 **estimate**) | $13.15 |
| Fleece blanket | from $12.84, $9.32 with Premium | ~$24.62 |
| Paid plan | Premium $24.99/mo yearly ($39 monthly): ~20% off typical items | Growth $24.99/mo, free after $12k/yr sales |

**Recommendation: start with Printify on the free plan.** It roughly halves your product cost on canvas and posters, and those are your money-makers. The trade-off is that Printify is a marketplace of different print shops, so quality varies by provider. To manage that:
1. For each product, pick a **US provider with high ratings** and order a **sample with Benji on it** (you'd want these photos anyway).
2. If a canvas sample disappoints, use Printful for canvases only. It does its own printing, so quality is more consistent, but it costs more.
3. Turn on **Printify Premium only when you spend more than about $125/month on base costs.** At ~20% off, that's where it pays for the $25 fee.
4. Both partners **reprint free** if their item arrives damaged or misprinted. Printful's policy: report within 30 days with a photo. Neither accepts "changed my mind" returns, so your listings must say *"made to order — no returns for change of mind; we fix any print or shipping problem."*
5. Fulfillment takes about **2–5 business days** before shipping. Promise roughly 7–12 business days total and beat it.

## 3. Etsy first, then Shopify

Start on Etsy. Open Shopify after ~30 orders and reviews, mainly for repeat customers, email list and booth customers. Etsy's processing fee varies slightly by method; the figures above are US standard.

## 5. AI tools and image rights
- **Midjourney:** Basic $10/mo, Standard $30/mo (unlimited "relax" generations). Commercial use is allowed on paid plans; companies over $1M revenue need Pro.
- **OpenAI gpt-image-1 (API):** about $0.04 (medium) to $0.25 (high, 1024x1536) per image. Good for photo-faithful edits of the actual pet.
- **Leonardo.ai:** free tier, paid from about $12/mo, with a commercial licence.
- **Workflow:** generate 4–8 options → pick the most faithful one (eyes, markings, ear shape matter most to owners) → fix details → upscale to print resolution. Check that the file is large enough for 16x20 before ordering a print.
- **Rights and rules:**
  - The customer must **own the photo or have permission**. Put "By uploading, you confirm you own this photo or have permission to use it" in the listing and in your first message.
  - **No copyrighted characters or brands** ("my dog as Spider-Man", Disney costumes, NFL logos). Politely decline, or offer a generic superhero cape.
  - Etsy asks sellers to be transparent about how items are made. **Say in the description that portraits are AI-assisted and finished by you.** Check Etsy's current Creativity Standards before you list.

## 6. Listings, SEO and photos
- **Title:** lead with what buyers type, e.g. "Custom Pet Portrait from Photo, Renaissance Dog Portrait, Personalized Pet Canvas Gift". Use all 13 tags with real phrases ("dog memorial gift", "cat portrait custom", "pet lover gift").
- **Photos:** Benji first, then 2–3 other pets (friends' pets, with permission), then a room mockup (Printify has a free mockup generator), then a "how it works" graphic (1 upload photo → 2 proof in 48h → 3 printed and shipped) and a size chart.
- **Personalization box:** "Pet name (optional) + style + any notes. After you order, send your clearest photo in Etsy messages: eye level, good light, face in focus."
- **Respond within a few hours.** Speed is a ranking and conversion signal, and the main thing customers mention in reviews.

## 7. Message templates (copy and edit)
**After the order:** "Hi {name}! Thanks for ordering a portrait of {pet} 🐾 Please reply with your clearest photo (eye level, good light, face in focus). By sending it you confirm it's your photo or you have permission to use it. I'll send a proof within 48 hours."

**Proof:** "Here's {pet}'s proof! Take a look at the eyes, markings and colors. Small tweaks are free; just tell me what to change. Once you approve, I'll send it to print (or deliver your file)."

**Revision done:** "Updated! I changed {x}. Happy with it, or should I adjust anything else?"

**Photo not usable:** "This photo is a bit blurry, so the portrait would lose detail. Do you have one where {pet}'s face is in focus? Even a phone photo near a window works great."

**Shipping or print problem:** "I'm so sorry it arrived like that. Could you send a photo of the issue? I'll get a free replacement printed right away." Then file it with your POD partner within 30 days.

**Memorial:** "I'm so sorry about {pet}. I'll take extra care with this one. If you have a favorite photo or a detail you want included (collar, toy), just let me know."

**Declining copyrighted characters:** "I can't use trademarked characters, but I can do {pet} as a royal portrait or a generic superhero with a cape. Want to see one?"

## 8. Florida legal and tax basics (not legal advice)
- **Etsy sales:** Etsy is a marketplace facilitator. It collects and remits Florida sales tax on Etsy orders, so you don't charge it there.
- **Selling direct (Shopify, market booths):** register with the Florida Department of Revenue (Form DR-1, free online) before you sell. Collect **7%** (6% state + Brevard 1% surtax) on physical prints sold to Florida customers. **Electronically delivered digital files are generally not taxable in Florida.**
- **LLC is optional:** Sunbiz filing is $125, then $138.75 annual report. A sole proprietorship is fine to start; consider an LLC once sales are steady. Don't use a trademark-style name you haven't checked.
- **Local:** Brevard County business tax receipt ~$37/yr. Also check whether the City of Melbourne requires a home-based business receipt.
- **Income tax:** report profit on Schedule C. Self-employment tax is 15.3% on net profit. Track every cost (samples, AI tools, fees). Etsy and Shopify issue 1099-Ks above the federal threshold.
- **Day job:** check your employer's outside-work or moonlighting policy.

## 10. 30-60-90 day plan (side-business hours)
**Days 1–30: build (≈8 h/week)**
- Wk 1: pick 3 styles; make Benji portraits in each; open the Etsy shop; read Etsy fee and AI rules.
- Wk 2: connect Printify; choose providers; order samples. Write listing copy and message templates.
- Wk 3: photograph samples; publish 8–10 listings (the table above); make 5 friend/family portraits for free in exchange for photos and honest feedback.
- Wk 4: first real orders; time yourself per portrait; fix anything slow.

**Days 31–60: first customers (≈6–10 h/week)**
- Aim for proofs within 48h, every time.
- Ask every buyer for a review. Reply to every message within a few hours.
- Post Benji process videos 2–3×/week (Instagram/TikTok/Pinterest).
- Add 5 more listings: two-pets, memorial variants, more printables.
- Test one price change at a time (e.g. canvas $69 vs $75 for 3–4 weeks), like the robot does.

**Days 61–90: decide and scale (≈8–10 h/week)**
- Review numbers: which products sell, your margin per order, hours per order.
- If you have 30+ orders: open Shopify (Basic $39/mo) and start an email list.
- Book one local market (Eau Gallie, Cocoa Village or a pet adoption event) with samples and a tablet for orders. Booth fees are often ~$50–100 (**estimate**).
- Start planning Q4 by early October: holiday listings, order cutoffs, more styles.

## 11. KPIs to track weekly (a simple spreadsheet is enough)
- Etsy listing views and **conversion rate** (orders ÷ visits; ~1–3% is a common range for handmade shops, **estimate**)
- Orders, revenue, **margin per order** after fees and POD costs
- **Proof turnaround** (target < 48h) and **revision rate** (target < 30%)
- **Refund or reprint rate** (target < 3%) and average review (target ≥ 4.8★)
- **Hours per order** (target < 30 minutes per custom portrait once practiced)
- Monthly profit ÷ hours worked (your real hourly rate)

## 12. What the game sim says (and how much to trust it)
200 simulated "Dom's real plan" companies over 3 years in Robot Founder, using the costs above:

| | Median peak annual revenue | Profitable over the last 12 months at year 3 |
|---|---|---|
| Founder not paid (like your side business) | ~$94k | 100% |
| Paying a $41.6k/yr founder salary (**estimate**) | ~$84k | ~56% |

In the sim, revenue came 54% from Etsy, 26% from Shopify and 20% from booths. Every run turned on Printify Premium.

The sim's robot works 10 hours a day, every day, and the traffic model is generic, not specific to pet portraits. **Read it as: "the unit economics work and fixed costs are tiny, so risk is low."** It is not a promise of $94k. The realistic risks are low traffic in a crowded niche and running out of evening hours, not losing money.
